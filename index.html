Frequently Asked 💭

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within Pakistan. Express delivery (1-2 days) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

Do you offer custom orders?

Yes! We specialize in custom name bracelets, custom phone charms, and personalized jewelry sets. Contact us on WhatsApp or Instagram DM with your design ideas.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across Pakistan and Sadapay. COD orders have a small handling fee of 100 PKR.

Can I return or exchange items?

Due to the handmade and personalized nature of our products, we don't accept returns or exchanges unless the item arrives damaged. Please inspect your order upon delivery and contact us within 24 hours with photos if there's an issue.

How do I measure my wrist size?

Use a flexible measuring tape or string around your wrist where you'd wear the bracelet. Add 0.5-1 inch for a comfortable fit. Check our size chart on product pages for detailed guidance.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we only ship within Pakistan. We're working on international shipping options for the future! Follow our Instagram for updates.
